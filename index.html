Is there a free demo class?

Yes. CSP Coaching offers a free demo class before admission, so you can see the teaching for yourself.

Which exams do you prepare for?

NDA, Army, Airforce, Navy, ICG, Delhi Police and State Police, SSC (CGL, CHSL, GD) and Railway (NTPC, Group D). We also run 8th–12th classes and tuition.

What are the class timings?

Defence 6:00–9:00 AM, Police 9:30 AM–12:30 PM, Railway 1:00–3:00 PM, SSC 5:00–8:00 PM and 8th–12th 4:00–7:00 PM.

Do you provide a hostel?

Yes. A safe hostel near the institute is available at ₹5,000 per month with no hidden charges.

Do you give study material?

Yes. PDF notes and printed study material are provided, along with weekly tests and mock exams.
